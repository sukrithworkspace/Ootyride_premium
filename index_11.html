Common questions

Do I pay on this website?

No. Booking only sends your details to our team on WhatsApp. Payment is discussed after they confirm your trip.

How do I cancel or change my trip date?

Tap “Cancel / change trip”, choose Cancel trip or Change trip date, enter your Booking ID or mobile number, and send the message on WhatsApp. Our team confirms it.

Is the estimate the final price?

No. It is a rough guide. The final price depends on route, vehicle, days and your requirements.

What should I carry in Ooty?

A light jacket, comfortable shoes and rain protection. Weather changes quickly, so check the forecast first.

Emergency help during your trip

Tap a number to call. In danger, call 112 first. You can also send your live location to our team.
